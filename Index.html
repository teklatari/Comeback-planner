CORE PRINCIPLES

Progressive overload

Each week adds load or reps — never the same workout twice.

Sport-first mindset

Every strength exercise has a direct tennis or volleyball purpose.

No skipping recovery

Thursday and Sunday protect joints coming back from rest.

Compound over isolation

Squats beat leg extensions. Rows beat curls. More bang per minute.

Train barefoot-ready

Flexible scheduling

If you miss a day, shift — don't double up or push through fatigue.

WEEKLY RECOVERY SCHEDULE

After Every Workout

  • 5 min cool-downWalk or easy movement — never stop abruptly
  • 10 min stretchTarget muscles worked that session
  • Foam rollWorked muscles only — 2 min total
  • Rehydrate500ml water minimum within 20 min

Sleep Optimization

  • 7–9 hoursNon-negotiable during a comeback. Growth hormone peaks in deep sleep.
  • Consistent scheduleSame bed and wake time — even weekends
  • No screen 1hr pre-bedBlue light suppresses melatonin by up to 3 hours
  • Cool room18–20°C is optimal for deep sleep and recovery
  • Post-workout nap20–30 min on heavy days dramatically speeds recovery

Pre-Workout 1–2 HRS BEFORE

  • GoalFuel performance without a full stomach
  • CarbsOats, banana, toast, rice — fast-digesting preferred
  • ProteinEggs, Greek yogurt, or whey shake
  • AvoidHigh fat, high fibre, or very large portions — causes sluggishness
  • Quick optionBanana + 2 boiled eggs + coffee
  • Skip ifTraining within 45 min of waking — just water + coffee is fine

Post-Workout WITHIN 45 MIN

  • GoalRepair muscle, replenish glycogen
  • Protein25–35g — critical window. Chicken, fish, eggs, dairy, or whey
  • CarbsRice, potato, bread, fruit — replace what you burned
  • HydrationAt least 500ml water immediately after
  • Quick optionGreek yogurt with oats + honey + banana
  • Don't skipPost-workout nutrition is when muscle is built — missing it wastes the session
